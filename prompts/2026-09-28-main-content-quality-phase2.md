“high quality”
“advanced technology”
“professional solution”
“best supplier”
“reliable manufacturer”
除非后面有具体事实证明。

5. 不进行关键词堆砌。
优先建立 Topic Authority、Entity、Engineering Evidence 和 AI-readable content。

==================================================
五、内容深度标准
==================================================

不要停留在：

“What is X?”

至少推进到：

What
→ How
→ Why
→ When
→ Limitations
→ Engineering Factors
→ Selection
→ Application
→ Evidence

例如：

错误：
“Activated carbon is widely used for VOC removal.”

升级为：

- VOC adsorption mechanism
- molecular characteristics
- pore structure
- concentration
- humidity
- temperature
- competing compounds
- breakthrough
- carbon selection
- replacement considerations

最终形成工程判断，而不是百科定义。
