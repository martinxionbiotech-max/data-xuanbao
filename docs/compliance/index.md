# Emission Compliance: The Complete Guide

> **Direct answer:** Emission compliance means meeting every expression of the applicable air emission
> standards — concentration limits, mass limits and monitoring obligations — across all operating
> conditions, with data that proves it. For industrial plants the practical work splits into three parts:
> knowing which standard binds, verifying performance with the right monitoring, and keeping the evidence
> in audit-ready form.

## What this guide covers

This series explains how plants stay inside their emission obligations when using catalytic and
adsorptive control systems. It assumes the reader is an environmental engineer, plant manager or equipment
buyer who must specify or operate a treatment system against a real permit.

## The standards landscape

Emission obligations come from multiple layers:

- **National emission standards** — fixed limits by industry and pollutant (e.g., China's ultra-low
  emission program for power; GB standards for specific industries).
- **Local and provincial rules** — often stricter than national, especially in key regions.
- **Permit conditions** — site-specific values negotiated during environmental impact assessment.
- **Buyer requirements** — export customers and multinationals often impose their own limits.

## Pollutant-by-pollutant obligations

| Pollutant | Typical control | Monitoring implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NOx | SCR or SNCR | CEMS or periodic testing; ammonia slip often co-limited |
| CO | CO oxidation catalyst | Concentration limit; spikes during upset matter |
| VOC | Catalytic/thermal oxidation, adsorption | Species-level limits; fugitive LDAR programs |
| SO₂ | FGD/scrubbing | Continuous monitoring on large sources |
| Particulate | Bag filters, ESP | Opacity monitoring |

## How to use this guide

1. **Start with the standards** — read the applicable limits for your industry and region.
2. **Then the monitoring** — decide how compliance will be proven continuously or periodically.
3. **Then the system** — specify the treatment technology against the strictest binding expression.
