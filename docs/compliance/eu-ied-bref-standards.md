# EU IED and BREF: The European Emission Framework in Brief

> **Part of the [Emission Compliance: The Complete Guide](index.md)** — this article is one of the detailed pages in the guide.

**Direct answer:** The EU Industrial Emissions Directive (IED) sets the European framework for industrial
air emissions: permits must reflect the Best Available Techniques (BAT) described in sector BREF documents,
with BAT-Associated Emission Levels (BAT-AELs) functioning as the reference limits. For plants selling
equipment or products into Europe, the BREF values are the design targets that matter.

## How the framework works

1. **IED** — the directive requiring large industrial installations to hold permits based on BAT.
2. **BREF documents** — sector reference documents (waste incineration, large combustion, chemicals,
   surface treatment) describing what BAT looks like in practice.
3. **BAT conclusions** — the binding part of each BREF, including BAT-AEL ranges.
4. **Permits** — national authorities translate BAT-AELs into installation-specific conditions,
   potentially stricter.

## BAT-AELs in perspective

| Sector | Relevant BREF | Typical focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waste incineration | WI BREF | NOx, HCl, SO₂, dust, dioxins, CO as completeness indicator |
| Large combustion | LCP BREF | NOx (SCR assumed for new large plants), SO₂, dust |
| Surface treatment (VOC) | STS BREF | VOC mass limits per solvent consumption |
| Chemicals | LVOC BREF | Species-level VOC and fugitive controls |

## What this means for equipment specification

- **NOx** — LCP BREF values effectively require SCR for new large coal/gas units; the catalyst and
  ammonia system are specified against the BAT-AEL, not a legacy limit.
- **CO** — incineration BAT-AELs treat CO as a continuous combustion-quality indicator; catalyst polishing
  systems must hold both average and short-term values.
- **VOC** — solvent-management-plan logic means destruction efficiency is judged against consumed
  solvent, changing how systems are specified and reported.

## Practical notes for buyers

- Check which BREF sector the installation falls under before specifying limits.
- BAT-AELs are ranges; the permit sets the number — obtain the permit first.
- Continuous monitoring obligations in the IED are heavier than in many other regimes; budget CEMS
  from day one.

## Manufacturer perspective

For export projects we design against the BAT-AEL, not the loosest regional number: a system that meets
Europe's framework meets most others. Where a customer supplies a permit, we treat the permit's strictest
expression as the design basis.
