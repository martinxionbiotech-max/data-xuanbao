# Activated Carbon Replacement Cycles and Regeneration Options

**Direct answer:** Activated carbon in VOC service typically reaches saturation in
months, not years — replacement or regeneration is a routine operating cost, not a
rare event. On-site thermal regeneration is rarely practical for small beds; most
plants replace the carbon and send the spent material for off-site reactivation,
while honeycomb carbons are generally single-use.

## Saturation and breakthrough

A carbon bed is spent when the outlet concentration breaks through the required
limit. Two saturation modes matter:

- **Full saturation** — the entire bed has reached equilibrium with the inlet
  concentration. The bed holds its maximum loading, but breakthrough happened long
  before this point.
- **Working capacity** — the loading actually usable before breakthrough. Design
  beds on working capacity (typically 10–25 wt% for common VOC, subject to
  species and conditions), not on equilibrium capacity.

## Replacement cycle estimation

Cycle time = working capacity × carbon mass ÷ VOC mass flow.

Practical ranges:

| Service | Typical cycle |
| --- | --- |
| Low concentration odour control | 6–24 months |
| Moderate VOC abatement | 3–12 months |
| High concentration / continuous | Weeks–months |
| Solvent recovery | Weeks, with frequent regeneration |

## Regeneration routes

**Off-site reactivation.** Spent carbon is thermally reactivated by specialists at
800–900°C, recovering most of the pore structure. Standard practice for granular
and columnar carbons; economics depend on transport distance and carbon value.

**On-site steam regeneration.** Practical mainly in solvent-recovery duty where
the desorbed solvent has value and the bed is engineered for it (closed loop,
condenser, decanter). Not practical for small abatement beds.

**Honeycomb carbon.** Generally single-use: the shaped substrate does not survive
reactivation economically. Design duties where replacement is simple and cheap
to execute.

## Managing the spend

- Track cumulative VOC loading, not just time in service.
- Sample the bed outlet weekly as breakthrough approaches.
- Keep a spare charge on site for the bed size — changeover in one shift.
- Contract reactivation logistics before the bed saturates, not after.

## Manufacturer perspective

We quote working capacity against the customer's actual VOC species and
concentration — the same carbon lasts 6 months on one stream and 18 on another.
Replacement planning belongs in the operating budget from day one.
